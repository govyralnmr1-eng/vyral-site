VYRAL Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

VYRAL is a social media automation project that allows users to connect supported third-party accounts, including TikTok, to authorized VYRAL and ARI features.

Information We Receive

When you connect a TikTok account through TikTok's authorization system, VYRAL may receive information that you explicitly authorize TikTok to provide. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include your TikTok account identifier, display name, profile picture, and other information covered by approved TikTok permissions.

How We Use Information

We use authorized account information only to provide the VYRAL features requested by the user, such as account connection, profile-related features, and approved social media workflows. VYRAL does not ask for your TikTok password.

Content Posting

If a user grants content-posting permissions, VYRAL may use those permissions to prepare, upload, or publish content to the connected account as directed by the user and as permitted by the relevant platform.

Data Storage and Security

VYRAL is designed to keep connection information and authorization credentials within the systems used to operate VYRAL. Reasonable measures are used to protect this information from unauthorized access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Third-Party Services

VYRAL relies on third-party services such as TikTok. Use of those services is also subject to their own privacy policies, terms, permissions, and platform rules.

Sharing of Information

VYRAL does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide an authorized integration or when required by law.

Access and Revocation

Users may disconnect or revoke a third-party account's authorization through the relevant platform where that option is provided. After access is revoked, VYRAL will no longer be able to use that authorization to access the account.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as VYRAL changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
